PocketCam Support

Support: 948026276@qq.com · Operator: Wang Hao

Getting started

Choose a style with the style button. A tap cycles through styles; hold and slide to choose using the style wheel. Use the adjacent strength control to adjust the amount. Pro provides more manual capture and color controls. The gear icon opens full-screen frosted Settings; tap Done to return to the camera.

What is free?

The included Blue White, Dark Tone and Sunlit styles, normal photo/video capture and style-strength adjustment are free. There is no daily photo quota.

How does Pro work?

Pro adds manual ISO, shutter speed, white balance and focus on supported cameras, detailed color tuning and reusable templates. An optional trial lasts 7 consecutive days after activation and does not automatically charge or renew. A separate one-time purchase unlocks Pro permanently. The current local price is shown before purchase. After the trial, free shooting continues and existing media/settings are retained.

Restoring a purchase

Use the Apple Account used for the purchase. Connect to the App Store, open Settings or the Pro offer and choose Restore Purchases. This restores eligible access and does not restart a used trial. If access is missing, send the exact screen message and app/iOS versions to support. Do not send payment-card details.

Refunds or purchase questions

Apple handles payment and refund requests. Review your purchase history and use https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for eligible purchases. Refund decisions are made through Apple's process. The Pro trial itself does not automatically charge.

Where are my captures?

Captures are kept in the app's local library. With Photos add-only permission, copies are also saved to the system library. The app does not browse all existing system photos. If saving to Photos fails, check available storage and permission in iOS Settings. Manage app-local copies and system-library copies separately; keep backups of important media.

Why is a control unavailable?

Available controls depend on the device, active camera and current capture state. Some settings are locked while recording, configuring or saving. Simulator sample mode cannot represent real camera controls.

Permissions and sound

Allow Camera for capture. Microphone is optional; declining it records silent video. Allow Photos add-only access to export captures. Open iOS Settings → Apps → PocketCam, or find PocketCam in Settings on older iOS versions.

Heat or slow preview

Stop recording and allow the device to cool if it becomes warm. To help investigate, report the device, iOS/App versions, selected camera and style, whether beauty was enabled, approximate session length and steps to reproduce. Performance depends on the device and conditions; the app does not promise a fixed temperature or frame rate.

Feedback and privacy requests

Use Settings → Feedback & Suggestions or email 948026276@qq.com. You can review feedback before sending; device details are optional and photos are not automatically attached. Include what happened and what you expected. Do not send passwords, identity documents, payment details or private photos. Use the same contact for requests about personal information in support correspondence.
