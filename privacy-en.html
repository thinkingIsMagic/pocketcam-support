PocketCam Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

PocketCam is operated by Wang Hao. For privacy questions or requests, contact 948026276@qq.com.

Camera and local processing

PocketCam processes camera images and color effects on your device. The current app does not operate a developer image-upload service or require an app account. Photos, videos, templates and preferences are stored locally. With permission, captures are also added to the system Photos library. Your device backup and photo-sync settings may copy them to services you control.

Optional beauty uses on-device face detection and temporary face-region information to render the effect. We do not use it to identify you, build a face-recognition profile, or send detected face information to a developer server. A saved photo can of course contain the face visible in that photo. Beauty is off by default.

Permissions

Camera permission enables preview and capture. Microphone permission enables audio during video recording; if declined, silent recording remains available. Photos add-only permission enables saving captures to the system library. The app's own library displays its locally saved captures and does not request access to read your entire system photo library. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payment and StoreKit transactions. PocketCam reads verified product and transaction records to provide Pro access, trial eligibility and restoration. A local time marker helps manage the trial period. We do not receive your payment-card details or maintain a developer purchase-account database in the current version. Apple processes its services under its own policies.

Optional feedback

A feedback draft stays on your device until you choose to send or share it. You can review the content first. It may include your message, contact details you enter, app version and, only if enabled, device and system version. Photos are not attached automatically. Email may also reveal your sender address and display name.

If you send feedback to our support contact, we use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain the app. We do not use support messages for advertising. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for these purposes and any applicable obligations, then delete it or remove identifying details. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion as applicable; we may need to confirm the request relates to you.

Your chosen email or sharing provider handles the content under its own terms. Support correspondence sent to the address above is processed by that email service, including storage and delivery. Depending on your and the service provider's location, it may be processed outside your country. This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even if a visitor is not signed in. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms or cookies to these pages. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of website visits.

Advertising, tracking and other services

The current app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. System services such as StoreKit, Photos sync, mail and sharing may make network connections. This policy does not replace those services' policies.

Your choices and local data

You can edit or clear an unsent feedback draft and manage app permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete captures in PocketCam and manage copies in Photos separately. Removing the app generally removes its local files, but does not automatically delete exported media, copies, backups or support messages already sent. A small local trial-timing record may remain in the device Keychain; it is not a cloud identity profile. For support data held by us, use the contact above.

The app is a general photography tool and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support contact, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. An App Store age rating does not imply special child-directed service features.

Security and updates

We use the platform's storage, permission and purchase-verification mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security. If our handling changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate in-app or published notice.
